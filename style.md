# Style guide

These rules apply to every chunk sent to the editor.

## Names — never alter the spelling of these

### Characters

- Aaron
- Bria
- Kathrine
- Flint
- Tobias
- Ruben
- Katja
- John
- Luna

### Places

- Great Oaks
- Iron Peaks
- Worldsea
- Central Rule
- Roak
- Meridian

> If you see what looks like a typo of one of these names, leave it alone.
> The author will review name spellings manually.

## Language standards (already enforced by the base prompt — repeated here for emphasis)

- American English spelling throughout (color, honor, center, gray, realize, organization, traveled, defense)
- Oxford comma for lists of three or more items
- Minimal corrections only — fix errors, do not rewrite for style

## Additional preferences

- "Worldsea" is one word, not "World Sea" or "World-Sea"
- Dialogue uses em-dashes — like this — with no surrounding spaces
- Internal thoughts are in _italics_
- Blackskull is a shipsname but _the_ is only before it in rare cases
- Blacksteel and Whitesteel are materials with these names
- Givers and Takers and Giving and Taking and Give and Take are always capitalized when refering to the powers. When in doubt, leave unchanged
- Kingsrings and Kingsring is a name of an object
- Tiranin people have awkward english on purpose
- The pirates have broken english on purpose.
